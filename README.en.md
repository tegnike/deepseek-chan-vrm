This is the VRM 1.0 model of DeepSeek-chan. It was made with the character modeling kit [Charakuru (きゃらくる)](https://github.com/nanocle/Charakuru).

## About DeepSeek-chan

DeepSeek-chan is a fan character based on the AI DeepSeek. To learn more about the character, see the "[DeepSeek娘](https://zh.moegirl.org.cn/DeepSeek%E5%A8%98)" article on Moegirlpedia (in Chinese).

## Model specifications

- VRM 1.0 with MToon materials
- Expressions: 18 presets (happy, angry, sad, relaxed, surprised, blinks, the mouth shapes for vowels, gaze, and more) and 23 part expressions that the presets are built from
- Gaze: driven by expressions (shape keys)
- Spring bones: 40 chains (hair, skirt, whale tail, ribbons) and 11 colliders
- Polygons: about 92,000 (almost all triangles)

## Made with Charakuru

This model was not made the usual way, where a person builds the shape in Blender or VRoid Studio. Knowing the differences below will make editing the model easier.

### How it differs

A typical VRM is made by a person who builds the shape in VRoid Studio or Blender, sets up the bones and weights, and converts it to VRM with UniVRM or VRM Add-on for Blender.

With Charakuru, a person and AI agents share the work, starting from a single character illustration.

1. An AI agent draws the head (with hair) and the body from four directions each, plus a full-body three-view drawing, based on the original illustration.
2. An AI agent operates [Tripo](https://www.tripo3d.ai/) to generate candidate 3D models of the head and body from those images. A person picks the candidates to use. Texture generation and rigging are also done in Tripo.
3. An AI agent operates Blender to separate and classify the parts and to set up the weights (transferred from a base body included in the kit), spring bones, and expressions.
4. A person checks the images, candidates, proportions, and motion in a local app and makes the decisions. No step requires a person to operate Blender directly.

The fixes made before release (expressions, blinking, fingers, skirt, tail, the gap at the base of the neck, and more) were also done by AI agents with Blender scripts.

### Things to know before editing

- **The mesh is a triangle mesh generated by AI.** It is not shaped with quads and edge loops like a hand-made model, so edits that rely on loop selection or subdivision are difficult.
- **The face and the back of the head are separate meshes.** `Head` covers only the front of the face, and `Hair` covers the back of the head. Without the hair, the back of the head is open.
- **The textures are 4K images generated per part.** The head, hair, upper body, accessories, and other parts each have their own texture. They are not painted on a single hand-arranged UV layout.
- **Spring bone parts are separate meshes.** Swinging hair strands, the skirt, the tail, and the ribbons are split into meshes named `Spring_xx_...`.
- **The expressions are shape keys made with scripts.** Shape keys on `Head` are registered as part expressions (`detail_...`), and the preset expressions combine them.
- **Production names remain.** The Blend file has scenes and collections whose names start with `CharaKit_`. Charakuru used them to manage production; they do not affect the VRM.
